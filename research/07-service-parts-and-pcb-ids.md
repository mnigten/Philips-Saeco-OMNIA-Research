## Research caution

A service supplier listing one PCB for several machine models does not necessarily mean a used donor PCB from any of those models is plug-and-play. Philips may supply a universal replacement that is programmed/configured during manufacture or service.

Always distinguish:
- bare PCB design number;
- populated PCB assembly;
- service part number;
- software/configuration variant;
- front-panel/mechanical assembly.
