# Research archive

This directory is the long-term research notebook for the Philips/Saeco OMNIA investigation.

The intention is to preserve not only conclusions, but also useful dead ends, competing hypotheses, upstream discoveries, protocol notes and the evidence behind decisions.

## Evidence levels

- **OBSERVED**: directly read, photographed or measured on our reference EP2230/10.
- **UPSTREAM**: demonstrated or documented by a cited external project.
- **CORROBORATED**: independently supported by multiple sources/projects.
- **INFERRED**: technically plausible conclusion from evidence, but not directly verified.
- **HYPOTHESIS**: question awaiting a controlled test.
- **REJECTED**: earlier idea disproved by later evidence.
